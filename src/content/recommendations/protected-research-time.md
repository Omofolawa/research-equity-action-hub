---
title: "Protected research time"
number: 4
recommendation: "Ensure that research time is formally allocated and genuinely protected from competing demands."
whyItMatters: "FAIR participants described research time being displaced by teaching, administrative and pastoral responsibilities. When research expectations remain high but time is repeatedly lost, opportunities to publish, develop funding applications, collaborate and progress can be affected."
whatCanBeDone:
  - "Audit workload models to ensure research time is explicitly ring-fenced."
  - "Review teaching allocations for early-career researchers."
whoShouldAct:
  - "Heads of School or Department"
  - "Senior university leaders"
  - "Research Directors"
relatedFindings:
  - "displaced-research-time"
---

Research time must be treated as a core component of the academic role, not a flexible buffer that can be absorbed by other tasks.
