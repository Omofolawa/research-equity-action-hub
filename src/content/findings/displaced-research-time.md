---
title: "Teaching heavy contracts and displaced research time"
order: 1
summary: "Participants described working in academic roles where teaching, administration and pastoral responsibilities could take priority over research."
whyItMatters: "Having research expectations without realistic and protected time to meet them can create unequal opportunities to develop a research career."
---

Although research time might formally appear within a workload, it was not always possible to protect that time in practice.

For some participants, this made it more difficult to publish, develop funding applications, build collaborations and maintain the sustained research activity expected for academic progression.
